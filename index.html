Questions, answered

PPF is a clear or matte urethane film applied over your paint. It absorbs stone chips and scratches, and light marks heal with heat.

PPF is physical armour against chips and scratches. Ceramic is a chemical shield that adds gloss and makes cleaning easy. Many owners combine both. We will recommend what fits your car and budget.

A full-body install usually takes 3 to 5 days. Front-end packages are faster. We confirm the timeline when you book.
